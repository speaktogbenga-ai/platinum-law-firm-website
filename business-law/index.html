Business law in Ontario can be complex and challenging, especially for those starting or running a business in the province. At Platinum Law Firm, our experienced business lawyers provide comprehensive legal advice and representation to help clients navigate this landscape with confidence. Whether you are starting a new business, seeking to protect your intellectual property, negotiating contracts, or dealing with disputes, our goal is to help you achieve your business goals and protect your interests.

Our lawyers have a deep understanding of the business environment in Ontario and are well-versed in the laws and regulations that govern this field. With our expertise and commitment to client satisfaction, you can be sure that you are in good hands. Contact us today to schedule a consultation and learn how we can assist you with your business law needs.

Our business law services include

  • Incorporation and post-incorporation
  • Partnerships
  • Business name registration
  • Contract negotiation, drafting and review
  • Purchase and sale of a business
  • Joint ventures
  • Business consulting
  • Minute book preparation
  • Not-for-profit organizations
  • Annual returns filing
