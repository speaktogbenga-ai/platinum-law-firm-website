Biography

Titilope Olatunji, commonly referred to as Titi, holds a Master of Laws degree from the University of Lagos. Her educational journey began with a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) degree from the University of Ilorin in Nigeria, followed by a Barrister-at-Law degree from the Nigerian Law School.

Since her admission to the Nigerian bar in 2011, Titi has accrued extensive experience in the practice of law, particularly in corporate/commercial law, civil litigation, and real estate law. In addition to her work in Nigeria, she is a member of the Law Society of Ontario.

Passion

Titi's driving force is to empower individuals in realizing their aspirations. She goes the extra mile to advocate for her clients.
